# Have the Native-sourced Wood cards read by Native readers before launch

Those Who Stayed Awake (Cherokee, from Mooney 1900) ships now, and the proposal names more North American Elders. The condition from the proposal: cards draw only from publicly shared stories, name the nation every time, prefer Native-authored sources, show no ceremonial objects or restricted ceremonies, and get a reading by Native readers before they ship.
